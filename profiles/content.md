# Content Profile

Version: 2.0.0

Use this profile for editorial, publishing, campaign, brand/style, channel, and reusable content-production work.

Create style guides, content strategy, editorial calendars, topic backlogs, publishing records, or equivalent durable artifacts lazily when Core is insufficient. Storage layout is implementation-specific.

Persist confirmed audience/positioning, durable style rules, content strategy, recurring workflows, publication state, campaign constraints, important source/evidence findings, and decisions that materially affect future output.

Draft chatter and abandoned wording SHOULD NOT be persisted unless the rejection captures a reusable constraint or decision.
