# Research Profile

Version: 2.0.0

Use this profile for research questions, source tracking, evidence synthesis, hypotheses, experiments, findings, and source-quality assessment.

Create question, source, evidence, experiment, or findings artifacts lazily when they add durable value beyond Core. Storage layout is implementation-specific.

Persist stable research questions, important sources, evidence-quality judgments, confirmed findings, falsified or supported hypotheses, methodological decisions, experiment outcomes, unresolved contradictions, and conclusions expensive to reconstruct.

Clearly distinguish evidence, inference, hypothesis, and decision. A finding SHOULD carry enough provenance to be re-evaluated later when practical.
