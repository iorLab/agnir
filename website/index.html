How it works

Discover. Load. Work. Checkpoint.

  1. DiscoverA fresh Executor starts from the authorized Project and finds its Agnir discovery record.
  2. LoadIt resolves the selected continuity and loads the Project truth needed for the current task.
  3. WorkThe Executor performs the real Project work outside Agnir Core.
  4. CheckpointMaterial changes are reconciled back into a coherent, durable Project-owned checkpoint.

Portable by design

Switch executors, not projects.

Agnir Core is neutral about Git, GitHub, filesystems, ChatGPT, specific Agents, Skills, and storage engines. Those are profiles, adapters, or distribution concerns around the continuity contract.
