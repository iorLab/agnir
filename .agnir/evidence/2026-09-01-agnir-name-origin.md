# Agnir name-origin verification — 2026-09-01

This evidence records the linguistic basis and Principal-approved product mapping for the project name `Agnir`.

## Linguistic basis

- Icelandic `ögn` means a tiny bit / particle.
- Its nominative plural is `agnir`.
- This was cross-checked on 2026-09-01 against:
  - dict.cc Icelandic-English entry for `ögn`: https://m.dict.cc/icelandic-english/%C3%B6gn.html
  - Wiktionary Icelandic entry for `ögn`, including the declension `ögn` -> nominative plural `agnir`: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%B6gn

These sources support the lexical claim only; they do not define Agnir product semantics.

## Product mapping

The Principal approved mapping the name to Agnir's existing durable-continuity model: Project truth is recoverable through small durable semantic pieces — Current State, Next Actions, Decisions, and Evidence / Checkpoints — that together allow a fresh compatible Executor to resume the Project without predecessor-private context.

This naming metaphor does not require any particular physical storage granularity or file layout.
