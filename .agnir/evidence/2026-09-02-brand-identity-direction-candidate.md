## Approved direction-level decisions

The Principal selected a synthesis of:

- **01 — Particle + Motion**;
- **03 — S / A Geometry**;
- **05 — Flow & Structure**.

For Agnir, the selected family direction is:

- role metaphor: **Structure Layer**;
- primary mark concept: structured **A-shaped geometry** expressed through discrete particles;
- current visual anchor: a prominent central particle within the A structure;
- brand-family relationship: shared particle-and-geometry grammar with Svif while remaining independently recognizable;
- palette direction: **sand / warm mineral family**;
- Svif counterpart: **Motion Layer** using a teal / turquoise palette.

## What is not approved yet

The current generated brand boards are concept/refinement evidence only and are **not production masters**. The following remain open:

- exact editable SVG geometry;
- exact A angle, aperture and negative-space construction;
- exact particle count, positions, scale progression and spacing;
- construction grid and clear-space rules;
- exact HEX/RGB palette values;
- monochrome, reverse and dark-background rules;
- final wordmark typeface/custom lettering and licensing;
- mark/wordmark lockup proportions;
- small-size simplification, if required after 16/32/64 px testing.

## Production-master gate

The next stage is deterministic vector reconstruction and rendered QA. A generated preview must not be silently converted into an approved master. Production master approval requires an editable vector source plus explicit review of geometry, palette, lockups and small-size behavior.
