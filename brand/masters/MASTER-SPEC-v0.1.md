# Agnir Logo Master Candidate v0.1

Status: **production master candidate — not locked**

This document converts the selected 01 + 03 + 05 concept direction into deterministic vector rules. It does not yet authorize README, Skill/distribution, favicon, PWA, social, or other production integration.

## Identity invariant

- Role: **Structure Layer / 结构层**.
- Core form: particle-built `A` geometry with a distinct central anchor.
- Family language: particles + geometry shared with Svif.
- The mark must communicate durable, discoverable pieces of Project truth without implying a database, repository host, storage backend, or execution surface dependency.

## Primary mark geometry

Source: `agnir-mark-v0.1.svg`

- ViewBox: `0 0 512 512`.
- Main `A` is constructed from fixed particle coordinates rather than text or a font glyph.
- Two particle legs establish the A silhouette; a light-particle inner layer and horizontal particle group establish structure; a larger central particle acts as the continuity anchor.
- Sparse outer particles provide the selected particulate-field character without becoming part of the A's minimum recognition geometry.
- SVG is self-contained and has no external image, stylesheet, or font dependency.

## Small-size candidate

Source: `agnir-mark-small-v0.1.svg`

The complete particle field becomes too fine at favicon scale. The small-size candidate preserves the A geometry as two sand-colored structural legs plus the central anchor particle.

Preliminary QA recommendation, pending Principal approval:

- `64px+`: primary mark may be used.
- `32px`: small-size variant preferred.
- `16px`: small-size variant required.

This is a separate approved-variant candidate, not permission to redraw or simplify the primary master silently.
