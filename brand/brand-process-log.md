## Brief

Agnir is a project-owned durable continuity protocol. The brand expresses durable, discoverable pieces of Project truth rather than a chat-history, database, repository-host, or storage-backend product.

Agnir and Svif remain independent brands with one visual family language: Agnir is the **Structure Layer / 结构层**; Svif is the **Motion Layer / 流动层**.

## Locked visual invariants

- Brand role: **Structure Layer / 结构层**.
- Core mark: structured particle-built A with a visible central anchor, exactly in the approved reference language.
- Palette: sand / warm-mineral family as displayed by the applicable approved board.
- Family grammar: particles + geometry shared with Svif while preserving separate brand recognition.
- Single-brand wordmark, lockup, light/dark/monochrome treatment, icon behavior, and other shown applications follow the Agnir single-brand board.
- Dual-brand/co-brand treatment follows the family board.
- Where boards differ, context-specific precedence in `brand/APPROVED-VISUAL-REFERENCE.md` controls; do not invent a merged interpretation.
